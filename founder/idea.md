# Autonomous Quant Trading Bot

- What it is: A self-improving algorithmic trading bot for US equities/ETFs that classifies market regimes, sizes risk, places bracket orders via Alpaca, and re-tunes its own parameters overnight via walk-forward optimization.
- Who it is for: Individual quant traders and small proprietary trading desks who want automated, regime-aware execution with institutional-grade risk controls and self-improving parameters.
- What it sells, at what price: Not yet commercialized. Target: SaaS subscription $200-500/month per account, or revenue share 10-20% of profits above benchmark.
- Where and how: Cloud-hosted (AWS/GCP) with web dashboard. Customers connect Alpaca paper/live accounts via OAuth. Distribution: direct B2B, quant community forums, Alpaca marketplace.
- Budget and constraints: Self-funded dev. Two Alpaca accounts (paper + live) for testing. No external capital. One developer. Regulatory: not a registered advisor; operates as software tool, not fund manager.
